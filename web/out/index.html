1. Quantitative Edge: Systematic Trend-Following

Positive Expectancy over 1,000 Trades
01.20-Day Breakout + Trend Filter

Markets exhibit fat tails (leptokurtosis). We enter long when daily close crosses the 20-day high and remains above both the 50-day and 200-day moving averages (structural bull regime). This filters out chop in sideways and bearish markets.

Rule: Long Entry if Close > Max(High, 20d) and MA50 > MA200.
02.2.0x ATR Invalidation Stop

Arbitrary percentage stops (e.g. 2% or 5%) fail because volatility changes constantly. We anchor our stop to 2.0x ATR₁₄. The trade is closed only when market price action objectively disproves the breakout thesis.

Formula: Stop Price = Entry Price - (2.0 × ATR₁₄).
03.Fixed 1% Risk-per-Trade (1R)

Position size is mathematically derived so that if a stop is hit, total portfolio capital drops by exactly 1.0% ($1R$). Ten consecutive losses result in only a 9.56% drawdown, shielding capital from ruin.

Formula: Shares = (Total Equity × 0.01) / (Entry - Stop).
04.Pyramiding Winners (Max 3 Tiers)

We never average down into losing positions. We only add into winning trades when unrealized profit exceeds +1.0R, simultaneously trailing the stop to break-even. Total symbol risk is capped at 2.0% ($2R$).

Rule: Add Tier 2/3 only if PnL ≥ +1.0R and stop moved to Entry.
05.Running Winner Immunity (INV-004)

When a trade catches a multi-month trend, its position size will naturally grow. The Guardian gate protects running winners: it will NEVER forcibly liquidate a winner to satisfy cash or rebalance rules.

Invariant: Winners run unencumbered until ATR stop is triggered.
06.80/15/5 Capital Allocation (INV-012)

Capital is ring-fenced into three inviolable sleeves: 80% Core Trend equities, 15% Reserve cash cushion (margin/slippage buffer), and 5% Black Swan defense vault strictly reserved for inverse/volatility ETPs.

Invariant: 80% Core / 15% Reserve / 5% Black Swan Vault.

2. Backend Mechanics: How TOSO Learns Without a Database

Deterministic Scientific Falsification
🛡️The 5-State Guardian Execution Gate

Before any order reaches the broker, it must pass three independent evaluation domains:

  • Domain 1 (Feed & Time): Verifies monotonic system clock, rejects stale quotes (>60s), and monitors broker ping (<15ms).
  • Domain 2 (Risk & Intake): Caps single-stock intake at 20%, bounds total portfolio heat to 6.0R, and enforces INV-004 winner protection.
  • Domain 3 (Execution Safety): Employs SHA-256 order idempotency keys to prevent duplicate fills and checks human arming authorization.
Fail-Closed Principle: If any check fails, the gate immediately blocks all live order placement while maintaining emergency cancel access.
⚡Mutual Exclusivity Invariant (INV-013)

Core trend equities (AAPL, MSFT, NVDA, AMZN, GOOGL) and Black Swan defensive ETPs (SH, PSQ, SPXU, VIXY, BIL) form mathematically disjoint sets.

The engine can never hold a long equity and its inverse hedge simultaneously. This prevents conflicting signals, self-hedging decay, and margin drag.

Mathematical Verification: Core Universe ∩ Defensive Universe = ∅ (Empty Set).
📜Merkle-Chained JSONL Journal (No SQL)

Instead of relying on relational or NoSQL databases that can suffer silent state drift, connection drops, or schema corruption, TOSO uses an append-only JSONL log on persistent disk (/app/evidence/).

Every trade fill, observation, and experiment is sealed with a cryptographic hash chained to the preceding entry: Hash_n = SHA256(ID_n | Seq_n | Timestamp_n | Payload_n | Hash_n-1). Any alteration invalidates the entire log.

Persistent Storage: Backed by Fly.io volume toso_data mounted at /app/evidence.
🧬Scientist Falsification & Atomic Adoption

Traditional AI models curve-fit to market noise. TOSO adapts via scientific hypothesis testing:

  • 1. Hypothesis: Isolates a falsifiable theory (e.g. adaptive stop width).
  • 2. Out-of-Sample (OOS): Tests variant on held-out historical data.
  • 3. Human Signoff (INV-014): Requires cryptographic operator signature.
  • 4. Atomic Adoption (INV-015): Mutates CoreConfig with a new SHA-256 version checksum with zero downtime.
Zero Black-Box Drift: Research can never autonomously mutate live trading without a human cryptographic seal.
